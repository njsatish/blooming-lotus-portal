Blooming Lotus Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

If you would like Blooming Lotus to delete personal information associated with your account, including your name, email address, phone number, and appointment history, you may request deletion at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Email njsatish@gmail.com with the subject line:

Data Deletion Request

Please send the request using the same email address used when booking appointments whenever possible.

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Appointment and booking history
  • Special request notes submitted through the website

Certain records may be retained if required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons.

Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

Users who signed in using Facebook may request deletion of their Blooming Lotus account data by emailing njsatish@gmail.com with the subject line:

Facebook Data Deletion Request

After identity verification, Blooming Lotus will remove applicable personal information and disconnect any Facebook account association stored by Blooming Lotus.

Contact

Questions about privacy or data deletion can be sent to: njsatish@gmail.com
