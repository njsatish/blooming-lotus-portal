Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Blooming Lotus ("we," "us") collects when you book an appointment or create an account through this site, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, preferred service, session length, and any special request notes you include.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook instead of creating a password, we receive your name and email address from that provider in order to create and identify your account. We do not receive your password, and we do not post to your Google or Facebook account on your behalf.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your appointments
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and reschedule notifications by email
  • To respond to questions or special requests you submit

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and account sessions

We use secure session tokens to keep you signed in between visits. These are used only to operate your account and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Data retention

We retain appointment and account information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@bloominglotus.denduluru.com.
